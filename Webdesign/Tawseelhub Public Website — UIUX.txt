# Tawseelhub Public Website — UI/UX Design Prompt

I want you to design a **new public marketing website for Tawseelhub**.

Domain:

**tawseelhub.com**

This is **not the internal application UI** and it is **not yet the future marketplace/storefront**.

The immediate purpose of this website is to represent Tawseelhub as a professional SaaS platform for delivery companies, explain the product clearly, show its major capabilities, communicate pricing, and convert visitors into Free users or Demo requests.

---

# 1. Product Positioning

Tawseelhub is much more than basic courier tracking software.

The core positioning should be:

## The Operating System for Delivery Companies

Tawseelhub connects the full business workflow of a delivery company:

**Trader → Order → Driver → Delivery → COD Collection → Driver Reconciliation → Expenses → Trader Settlement → Accounting → Payroll → Reports**

The major difference from many delivery-management competitors is that Tawseelhub covers both:

### Operations

* Orders
* Traders
* Customers
* Drivers
* Assignments
* Delivery statuses
* Returns
* Collections
* Expenses
* Mobile operations

### Finance & Administration

* Driver reconciliation
* Trader settlements
* COD management
* Accounting
* Driver commissions
* Employee payroll
* Financial reports
* Operational reports
* Audit/history

The website must communicate that Tawseelhub is:

**More complete, easier to use, and designed around how delivery companies actually operate every day.**

Do NOT position Tawseelhub mainly around generic claims such as:

* AI-powered logistics
* revolutionary technology
* intelligent innovation
* smart transformation

unless the specific capability actually exists.

We want the product to look practical, complete and easy to use.

# 3. Design Direction

Create a clean modern SaaS website.

Preferred visual direction:

* White / very light background
* Strong blue/navy primary identity
* Modern typography
* Plenty of whitespace
* Soft shadows
* Rounded but professional cards
* Clean icons
* Very limited gradients
* Avoid excessive decorative illustrations
* Avoid generic stock photos of smiling couriers
* Avoid visual clutter

The design should look closer to a modern B2B SaaS company than a traditional logistics website.

It must feel simple even though the application itself contains many powerful features.

---

# 4. Very Important — Do NOT Overload the Homepage

The previous concept became too congested.

Do NOT place the entire application on the homepage.

Do NOT create a huge fake dashboard containing:

* multiple charts
* recent activity
* top traders
* top drivers
* COD cards
* maps
* many widgets

Tawseelhub does NOT need a separate special marketing dashboard fabricated just for the website.

Instead:

Use **one small clean product screenshot/mockup** in the hero.

Preferably show a real or realistic Tawseelhub application screen such as:

* Main dashboard
* Orders list
* Order management screen

The screenshot should demonstrate that the product exists without dominating the whole page.

Later sections can show individual screens separately.
